Ecommerce Development: common questions

Which platform is best?

Shopify suits most brands and WooCommerce suits WordPress-based stores. We recommend after a short call.

Can you migrate my store?

Yes, including products, customers and redirects.

Shopify or WooCommerce: which is better?

Shopify is quick to launch and easy to manage, while WooCommerce offers more control on WordPress. We recommend one after looking at your catalogue, budget and growth plans.

Can you migrate my existing store?

Yes. We can move products, customers and content to a new platform and plan redirects so you keep your search visibility.

Which payment methods can I accept?

Most major options can be connected, including UPI, cards, net banking and wallets, depending on the platform and payment provider.

Do you help with marketing after launch?

Yes. SEO, ads, email and social media can all support the store once it is live, so it starts getting traffic and orders.
