What affects the cost of a project

Packages give a starting point. These are the things that move a quote up or down.

Scope and number of services

Running SEO alone costs less than SEO, ads and social media together.

Competition in your market

Crowded markets usually need more content, links or ad budget to stand out.

Pages and features

A five-page site and a large catalogue with integrations are very different projects.

Ad spend

The money paid to Google or Meta for clicks is separate from our management work.

Content and creative volume

The number of posts, articles and designs each month changes the workload.

Reporting and support level

Monthly reports and bi-weekly reviews with a dedicated manager need different amounts of time.
