MOONNEXT - connect the website forms to your Google Sheet (about 3 minutes, one time)

Your sheet: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HyFTU3qNKM1O5hqE9FFkxISk08U7qyzDeoNTMNha1bc/edit

Which forms save to it: the popup that every button opens, the contact-page form, the short form in the hero of the 12 service pages, and the footer newsletter box.
The "Source" column shows which page and button each lead came from.

STEPS
1. Open the sheet above, signed in with the Google account that owns it.
2. Click Extensions > Apps Script. Delete everything in the editor.
3. Open apps-script/Code.gs from this folder, copy ALL of it and paste it into the editor. Click Save.
4. Click Deploy > New deployment. Click the gear icon > "Web app".
      Execute as:      Me
      Who has access:  Anyone
   Click Deploy. When Google asks, click Authorize access > choose your account > Advanced > "Go to project" > Allow.
5. Copy the "Web app URL" (it ends with /exec).
6. Open js/config.js, find   SHEETS_ENDPOINT: '',   and paste the URL between the quotes:
      SHEETS_ENDPOINT: 'https://script.google.com/macros/s/XXXXXXXX/exec',
7. Upload the site again.

TEST
- Open the /exec URL in a browser. You should see:  {"ok":true,"service":"MOONNEXT lead endpoint"}
- Fill any form on the live site. A new row appears in the first tab of the sheet within a few seconds.

IF NOTHING ARRIVES
- "Who has access" must be Anyone (not "Only myself").
- After you ever edit Code.gs: Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon > Version: New version > Deploy.
- Until SHEETS_ENDPOINT is filled in, forms show "not connected yet" and offer a prefilled WhatsApp message, so no lead is lost silently.
