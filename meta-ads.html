Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram): common questions

Do Meta Ads work for B2B?

Yes, especially for lead generation with a strong offer and instant forms.

How often are creatives refreshed?

Typically every two to three weeks, based on frequency and results.

Should I choose Meta Ads or Google Ads?

Google Ads catches people who are searching now, while Meta Ads creates interest before they search. Many businesses run both, and we suggest a split after reviewing your goals.

What do I need to start Meta Ads?

A business page, a way to track enquiries and a clear offer. We help set up the ad account, pixel and tracking if they are missing.

How important is creative?

Very. On Facebook and Instagram the image or video does most of the work, so we plan creative variations and test them instead of relying on one version.

Can Meta Ads send leads to WhatsApp?

Yes. Campaigns can start WhatsApp conversations or collect leads through forms, depending on what suits your business.
