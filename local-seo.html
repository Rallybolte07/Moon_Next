Local SEO: common questions

Is Local SEO only for shops?

No. Clinics, consultants, schools and service businesses all gain from it.

How fast does it work?

Profile fixes can lift visibility in weeks, and rankings stabilise over two to three months.

What is a Google Business Profile?

It is the free listing that appears on Google Search and Maps with your address, hours, photos and reviews. For local businesses it is often the first thing a customer sees.

How is Local SEO different from regular SEO?

Regular SEO targets searches from anywhere. Local SEO targets people near you, using your profile, reviews, listings and location pages.

Do I need a shop for Local SEO?

You need a real business address or a defined service area. Businesses that travel to customers can still use Local SEO by setting their service areas.

Can you manage reviews for me?

We guide how to request reviews and help draft replies. Reviews must come from real customers, and we never post fake ones.
