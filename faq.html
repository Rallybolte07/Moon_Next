Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about working with MOONNEXT.

Get Free Consultation
What services does MOONNEXT offer?

SEO, local SEO, Google and Meta ads, social media, website design and development, ecommerce, content, email marketing and branding.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most sites see movement in 60 to 90 days, with stronger growth from month four or five.

Do you guarantee rankings or sales?

No. We commit to transparent work, clear targets and honest reporting.

What is the minimum ad budget?

It depends on your market. We recommend a budget after a free audit.

Do I own my ad accounts and website?

Yes. Every account, asset and piece of data belongs to you.

How do you report results?

A monthly report in plain language plus a call to walk through it.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We can optimise it or rebuild it, whichever gives better returns.

How long does a website project take?

Typically three to six weeks, depending on pages and features.

Do you build ecommerce stores?

Yes, on Shopify, WooCommerce or custom stacks.

Do you create content and creatives?

Yes. Our team handles copy, design, video planning and scheduling.

Is there a long-term contract?

No lock-in. Most clients stay because the results are clear.

Which industries do you work with?

Real estate, healthcare, education, ecommerce, SaaS, finance and hospitality, among others.

Do you offer a free audit?

Yes. Request a free website and marketing audit from the contact page.

How do we get started?

Share your goals through the contact form. We reply within one working day.

Do you use AI tools?

Yes, for research and analysis. Strategy and final content are always reviewed by people.

Can I start with one service and add more later?

Yes. Many clients begin with one channel, such as SEO or ads, and add others once it is working. We review the plan every month.

Can I run SEO and ads together?

Yes, and many businesses do. Ads bring leads quickly while SEO builds lasting traffic. We plan the split of budget and effort around your goals.

What should I prepare before our first conversation?

Your website address, a short description of your business and customers, your main goal and, if you have one, a rough budget. If you are not sure about any of these, we help you work them out.

What happens in the free audit?

We review your website, search visibility, competitors and goals, then share what we found and where we would start. The audit is delivered within 48 hours.

Will my website work well on mobile phones?

Yes. We design and build every page to work on phones, tablets and desktops, and we test key pages on real devices before launch.

What is a Google Business Profile and do I need one?

It is the free listing that shows your business on Google Search and Maps. If customers find you locally, it is one of the most useful things you can set up, and our Local SEO service covers it.

Do you work with startups and small businesses?

Yes. The first package on our pricing page is made for new businesses, and we scale the scope to your goals and budget.

How can I contact you quickly?

Use the contact form, call us or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within one working day.
