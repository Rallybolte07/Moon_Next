Quick wins you can apply this week

Six small changes that often make a visible difference. No agency needed.

Complete your Google Business Profile

Add the right categories, opening hours, services and photos so local searchers can trust you.

Add negative keywords to search ads

Block searches that never turn into customers so budget goes where it counts.

Put one clear action above the fold

Show a call, form or WhatsApp button as soon as the page loads.

Check your mobile page speed

Compress large images first, since they are the most common reason pages feel slow.

Track calls and form submissions

Install conversion tracking so you know which pages and campaigns produce leads.

Ask recent customers for a review

A short message with your review link after a good experience is often enough.
