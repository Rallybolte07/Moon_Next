Graphic Design & Branding: common questions

What files will I receive?

Editable source files plus PNG, SVG and PDF exports.

How many logo concepts?

Three concepts, then refinement of the one you choose.

Do I get editable files?

We agree the file formats at the start and deliver brand assets in the formats you need for print and digital use. Your brand assets belong to you.

Can you redesign my existing logo?

Yes. We can refresh your current logo while keeping what people already recognise, or create something new if you want a fresh start.

How many concepts will I see?

We agree the number of concepts and revision rounds up front, so the process stays clear and on schedule.

Can you design for both print and social media?

Yes. We create print-ready files and social media creatives from the same brand system, so everything matches.
