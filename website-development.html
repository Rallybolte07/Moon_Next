Website Development: common questions

Do you build on WordPress?

Yes, and also custom-coded sites depending on your needs.

Can I edit content myself?

Yes, we train your team and share documentation.

Which technologies do you use?

We choose the stack to fit the project, from WordPress and Shopify to custom code. The goal is a site you can manage easily and that performs well.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We set up a content management system and show your team how to edit pages, text and images.

Do you help with hosting and domains?

We can advise on hosting and help with domain and DNS setup so the launch goes smoothly. Your accounts stay in your name.

What happens after launch?

We check that everything works as expected once the site is live. Ongoing support, updates and improvements can be arranged as a separate service.
