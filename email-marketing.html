Email Marketing: common questions

Which email tool do you use?

Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Zoho or Brevo, depending on your stack.

Is it compliant?

Yes. We follow consent and unsubscribe best practice.

Do I need an email list to start?

Not necessarily. We can help you build one with sign-up forms and offers, and make better use of the contacts you already have, in line with consent rules.

Which email tools do you work with?

We use established email platforms and recommend the one that suits your list size, budget and the tools you already use.

How do you keep emails out of spam?

We use permission-based lists, clear sender details, sensible sending habits and clean formatting. Deliverability depends on many factors, so we monitor it over time.

How often should I send emails?

It depends on your audience and offers. We start with a rhythm you can sustain, then adjust based on opens, clicks and unsubscribes.
